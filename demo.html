📖 How to Use

  1. Select Your Game: Choose which Pokemon game you're playing from the dropdown menu
  2. Track Your Progress: Click on gym badges as you earn them to unlock new Pokemon encounters
  3. Build Your Team: Click the "+" slots to open a modal and add Pokemon to your team
  4. View Suggestions: Check the suggestions section for smart recommendations based on your current team
  5. Analyze Coverage: Review the type coverage grid to ensure your team is well-balanced
  6. Toggle Dark Mode: Click the sun/moon button in the header to switch between light and dark themes
